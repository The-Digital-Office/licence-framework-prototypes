M365 Licence Audit and Allocation Framework

Organisational Baseline Tool

A guided three-person conversation. The CISO or security lead, the Information Governance lead, and the head of IT answer each question Yes or No for the council. The tool translates your answers into the licence stack that goes to the allocation tool.

Prototype for comment (v1.3). One session, three people. The output is the baseline reference used by every allocation and audit for the year ahead.

Session context

Council-wide requirements 0 of 20 answered

For every question, decide whether this capability should apply to every licensed user at your council. Answer in the context of what the council wants to enforce, not what any specific team or system currently does.

Requirements for all users that the questions above didn't cover. The allocation tool will pass these on to the service desk as manual notes.

Reminder on compliance toolsIf your Yes answers include sensitivity labels, DLP, records management, eDiscovery or insider risk (any of the "information protection" or "records and investigation" questions), every user whose content is covered needs the licence that delivers it. Not just the users of the tools themselves. The IG lead should think about scope.

Special groups

Groups that need capabilities beyond what everyone gets. Typical examples: privileged administrators, the Information Governance team, finance staff dealing with sensitive data, members of a legal team. Add one per group. For each, you'll only see the questions you answered No above, because everything you said Yes to already applies to them.
